# Public homepage — claim register

Not a strategy document. A safeguard.

Every substantive claim on the public homepage is listed here with the evidence
behind it and the wording it may **not** be escalated into. LunchBox Connect has
no client base yet, so the page has to earn credibility from the size of the
problem and the seriousness of the operating model — never from implied scale.

If you are about to add a customer count, a logo wall, a testimonial or a
regulator's approval to the homepage: none of those exist. Read §Prohibited
below before writing anything.

**TYPE** is one of `OFFICIAL UAE FACT`, `CURRENT PRODUCT CAPABILITY`,
`RESEARCH-SUPPORTED POSITION`, `FUTURE VISION`.

---

## 1. 331 Early Childhood Centres · 233 private schools

- **TYPE** — OFFICIAL UAE FACT
- **EVIDENCE** — KHDA, 24 August 2025, on Dubai's 2025–26 private education
  sector.
- **ALLOWED WORDING** — the two numbers, each shown beside `Dubai, KHDA
  2025–26`, with a source line naming the date.
- **DO NOT ESCALATE TO** — a live or current count; a UAE-wide figure; a market
  size; a number of prospects, leads or addressable customers; "the UAE has
  331…" without Dubai and the year.

## 2. 456 educational institutions monitored annually for food safety

- **TYPE** — OFFICIAL UAE FACT
- **EVIDENCE** — Dubai Municipality, September 2025: 456 educational
  institutions overseen annually by its dedicated food-safety inspection team,
  alongside field inspections, DM Checked monitoring, menu review and Smart
  Food Choices requirements.
- **ALLOWED WORDING** — the number, the phrase "monitored annually for food
  safety", the attribution `Dubai Municipality, 2025`.
- **DO NOT ESCALATE TO** — LunchBox Connect being integrated with DM Checked;
  LunchBox Connect performing, supporting or guaranteeing regulatory approval;
  any suggestion these 456 are customers or that inspection outcomes are
  visible in the product.

## 3. ADEK Food and Nutrition context

- **TYPE** — OFFICIAL UAE FACT
- **EVIDENCE** — ADEK has current Food and Nutrition policies for schools and
  Early Education Institutions; the school policy includes requirements around
  records of food allergies and intolerances, and allergen warnings.
- **ALLOWED WORDING** — that food, nutrition, records and operational
  expectations are increasingly structured; ADEK named only as market context.
- **DO NOT ESCALATE TO** — "ADEK approved"; "compliant with ADEK"; any medical,
  clinical or allergy-safety claim; any suggestion the product interprets
  allergy information or makes a clinical decision.

## 4. ADEK Parent Engagement context

- **TYPE** — OFFICIAL UAE FACT
- **EVIDENCE** — ADEK Parent Engagement / Parent Engagement and Communication
  frameworks.
- **ALLOWED WORDING** — support for treating Parents as an important
  stakeholder in the information chain.
- **DO NOT ESCALATE TO** — a mandate that Institutions must use software; a
  claim that LunchBox Connect satisfies an ADEK requirement.

## 5. Dubai Education 33 context

- **TYPE** — OFFICIAL UAE FACT
- **EVIDENCE** — Dubai Education 33 is learner-centric and includes engaged
  educators and Parents as part of the education ecosystem.
- **ALLOWED WORDING** — one short contextual line under Today/Tomorrow.
- **DO NOT ESCALATE TO** — participation in, selection for, or alignment
  certified by the initiative.

## 6. UAE National Food Security Strategy 2051 context

- **TYPE** — OFFICIAL UAE FACT
- **EVIDENCE** — the strategy includes technology-enabled food systems,
  improving nutrition, food safety and reducing food waste.
- **ALLOWED WORDING** — national context only, one short line.
- **DO NOT ESCALATE TO** — LunchBox Connect being a government initiative, a
  national platform, a national standard, or public infrastructure.

## 7. UAE personal-data obligations

- **TYPE** — OFFICIAL UAE FACT
- **EVIDENCE** — the UAE Personal Data Protection framework creates obligations
  around personal-data governance and confidentiality.
- **ALLOWED WORDING** — nothing is currently claimed on the homepage. The page
  states that internal operations stay private and Parent visibility is
  controlled, which describes product behaviour, not legal status.
- **DO NOT ESCALATE TO** — "PDPL certified", "PDPL compliant", or any
  certification the company does not hold.

## 8. Managed meal service powered by its own operating system

- **TYPE** — CURRENT PRODUCT CAPABILITY / RESEARCH-SUPPORTED POSITION
- **EVIDENCE** — the operating chain exists in the product through migrations
  `0048`–`0055`: Student Meal Plan entitlement, dietary requirement and Special
  Meal resolution, exact Demand and finalisation, production and packing,
  delivery configuration, manifests, dispatch, handover, issues and bounded
  correction.
- **ALLOWED WORDING** — "combines a managed meal service with the operating
  system behind it"; "operates the meal service while its platform connects
  multiple Institutions through one controlled operational model".
- **DO NOT ESCALATE TO** — a proven standalone SaaS market; a multi-caterer
  ecosystem in the present tense; "the first", "the only", "market leader",
  "leading UAE provider"; any commercial-outcome claim such as "pays for
  itself", "revenue line" or "margin line".

## 9. Current Parent capability

- **TYPE** — CURRENT PRODUCT CAPABILITY
- **EVIDENCE** — the real Parent portal: `ParentHome` (today's meals for the
  selected child, meals completed today, recent days), `ParentMenu` (this
  week's published menu with dish detail), `ParentInsights` (average intake,
  meals recorded, intake bands and trend), `ParentProfile`.
- **ALLOWED WORDING** — "when access is provided through the Institution,
  Parents see their own child's published meals and the intake information
  recorded for that child"; "the Parent is part of the information chain — not
  the internal operations chain".
- **DO NOT ESCALATE TO** — live or real-time tracking; Parents ordering,
  changing or cancelling meals; Parents editing dietary requirements; a
  nutrition score or any invented metric; "peace of mind", "stress free",
  "happier children". The homepage visual is an ABSTRACT representation derived
  from those screens — it must never be presented as a screenshot, and must
  carry no child name and no fabricated figure.

## 10. Current Kitchen and delivery capability

- **TYPE** — CURRENT PRODUCT CAPABILITY
- **EVIDENCE** — exact Demand from published service and child entitlement;
  production and packing states; Special Meal lines; manifests, Driver
  assignment, dispatch and recorded handover; operational issues and
  reconciliation. Covered by the browser and SQL suites.
- **ALLOWED WORDING** — "turn published service and child entitlement into
  exact Demand, production, packing, dispatch and recorded handover".
- **DO NOT ESCALATE TO** — temperature or cold-chain enforcement; batch/lot
  traceability; regulator-approval statuses; route optimisation; inventory or
  procurement; multi-kitchen routing. All are deliberately out of scope and
  none exists.

## 11. Current Institution capability

- **TYPE** — CURRENT PRODUCT CAPABILITY
- **EVIDENCE** — Institution-level service configuration, Students and staff,
  published service, dietary requirements, receiver management and delivery
  handover.
- **ALLOWED WORDING** — "see and manage the parts of the meal programme that
  belong to your Institution".
- **DO NOT ESCALATE TO** — Institution Admin assigning Student Meal Plans.
  Student Meal Plan assignment is Super Admin authority; the homepage must not
  imply otherwise.

## 12. Current Classroom capability

- **TYPE** — CURRENT PRODUCT CAPABILITY
- **EVIDENCE** — the Classroom screen shows only applicable published Meal
  Periods and the children in that class, and records intake and exceptions.
- **ALLOWED WORDING** — "see the children and Meal Periods that actually apply,
  then record what happened without exposing the Kitchen's internal operation".
- **DO NOT ESCALATE TO** — Classroom staff publishing notes, changing
  entitlement, or seeing Kitchen production data.

## 13. A possible common operating layer

- **TYPE** — FUTURE VISION
- **EVIDENCE** — none. This is an ambition, conditional on market validation.
- **ALLOWED WORDING** — must stay conditional and must sit under a heading that
  says **Tomorrow**: "if direct market validation supports it, the same
  architecture can evolve to connect additional kitchens, caterers and
  education groups". The category line "building the connected operating
  infrastructure for institutional child nutrition" is a company ambition.
- **DO NOT ESCALATE TO** — present tense; "national platform"; "UAE standard";
  "government infrastructure"; "national operating system"; adopted, selected
  or endorsed by anyone.

## Prohibited outright — no evidence exists

No client base exists, so the homepage carries **zero** social proof. Do not add:

client logos · customer counts · testimonials · case studies · "trusted by" ·
"used by leading nurseries" · "serving thousands of children" · meals-served
counters · review scores · customer-success statistics · ROI or retention
statistics · partner, investor, government or regulator logos · certifications
the company does not hold.

And do not reintroduce this speculative sales language from older outreach
material:

"the program pays for itself" · "revenue line" · "margin line" · "groups tell
us" · "our clients tell us" · "eliminates allergy risk" · "allergy safe" ·
"fully compliant" · "government approved" · "KHDA approved" · "Dubai
Municipality approved" · "ADEK approved" · "no other company does this" · "the
first" · "the only" · "market leader" · "leading UAE provider".

`src/pages/landing.claims.test.tsx` fails if any of the forbidden strings
reaches the rendered homepage. That test is the enforcement; this file is the
reason.

## Framing that must not drift

The UAE problem statement is about **operational boundaries**, not about anyone
being bad at their job. Permitted: the sector is growing and expectations around
food, wellbeing, records and Parent engagement are becoming more structured, and
one child's meal crosses many hands. Not permitted, because it has not been
proven: "UAE nurseries are badly run", "all UAE schools use spreadsheets", "the
UAE system is fragmented".
